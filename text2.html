Crafted with Passion, Served with Heart

Bean & Bloom was born from a simple dream: to create a space where coffee isn't just a beverage, it's an experience. Since 2018, we've been roasting exceptional beans and building a community of coffee lovers who appreciate the art and science behind every cup.

Our founder, Sofia Martinez, spent five years traveling through coffee farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, learning from master roasters and forging relationships with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.
